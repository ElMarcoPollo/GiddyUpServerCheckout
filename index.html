2. Servers and cash payouts

Enter everyone’s hours. Choose the report preparer, then enter cash payouts. Everyone else receives whole dollars; only the preparer may receive coins. Cash payouts reduce paycheck amounts.

When a split leaves a fractional cent, the app assigns remaining cents to the largest fractional shares, with ties going to the preparer.This keeps other entered cash payouts and assigns the remaining cash to the preparer, up to their earnings.
